# Decision-driver research

Find out what buyers actually decide on, then check whether the free or trial experience proves it.

## 1. Define the decision

Name the decision: adopt the category, switch from a competitor or manual process, upgrade from free, or expand to the team. Each has different drivers. Interview the people who just made it:

- Adoption: recent adopters.
- Switching: recent switchers and people who evaluated but stayed.
- Upgrade: recent converters and active free users who haven't converted.
- Expansion: team admins and champions.

## 2. Discover drivers (interviews)

Ask about the last real decision, not opinions:

1. What made you start looking? What did you do first?
2. Who else was involved?
3. What did you compare, and how did you check each option?
4. What would have ruled an option out immediately?
5. Why did you pick what you picked? What was second, and why not that?
6. What almost tipped it the other way?
7. How did you justify it to your boss in one sentence?

Extract every driver mentioned, how many people raised it, which ones carried emotion, and which were deal-breakers. Note drivers you didn't expect.

## 3. Rank drivers (survey)

Ask respondents to rate each driver's importance on a 1–7 scale. Read the mean and spread together:

- High mean, low spread: table stakes. Must be visible in the free experience, but won't differentiate.
- High mean, high spread: important to some segments. Split by company size, role, use case and current solution to find who.
- Middling mean, high spread: a segmentation signal. Consider segment-specific onboarding.
- Low mean, low spread: don't spend free-experience effort here.

For the top drivers, ask what the driver means to them, what proof they look for, and what makes a claim about it believable.

## 4. Map drivers to the free experience

| Driver | Can free/trial prove it? | How | Fix if not |
|---|---|---|---|

Then answer:

- Does activation prove the #1 driver?
- Which drivers need time, a whole team, or a human to prove? Those shape trial length, team invites, social proof, or a sales-assist step.
- Does the upgrade trigger fall where paid features serve a top driver?
