# How plg-growth skills reason and report

House rules for every skill in this plugin. You already know issue trees, MECE, hypothesis trees and the Minto pyramid. Apply them silently. Do not explain or walk through them in the output.

1. **Answer first.** Open with the recommendation or verdict in one or two sentences. Then the few reasons. Then the data.
2. **Guess before you dig.** Name where you think the problem is and what evidence would prove the guess wrong. Collect that evidence, not everything.
3. **Separate direct and indirect drivers.** Direct drivers are the arithmetic that defines a metric (signups × activation × paid conversion × ARPA). Indirect drivers are behaviours that move them (setup friction, perceived value). Size opportunities with the arithmetic; argue the behaviours.
4. **Pick 2–3 branches.** Rank by how much the outcome moves if the driver moves a realistic amount, then by effort. Seven priorities means none.
5. **Unknown is a finding.** A metric the team cannot measure is often the biggest lever. Say so and make measuring it a work item. Never fill the gap with a made-up benchmark.
6. **No orphan tasks.** Every action tests or exploits a named hypothesis and says what result would prove it right.
7. **Numbers need a source.** Use the user's own baseline and trend. Quote an outside benchmark only with its named source; otherwise write "measure your own baseline". No invented scores, scorecards, or confidence percentages.
8. **Short by default.** Answer a narrow question directly. Run the full analysis only when the user asks for it or a short answer would mislead. Offer the deeper pass in one line at the end.
