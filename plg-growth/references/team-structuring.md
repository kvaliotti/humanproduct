# Growth team structure

Used by `plg-revenue-analysis` and `plg-transformation`. Pick the levers first, then the structure. Never the reverse.

## Every growth team needs three things

1. **One owner per lever.** A PM accountable for the number, not a committee.
2. **Enough people to ship on its own.** PM, design, engineering and analytics. A growth PM with no engineers is a person with opinions.
3. **A lever, not "growth".** The team's goal is one branch of the revenue driver tree.

## Choosing a structure

| Situation | Structure |
|---|---|
| One growth PM, or one lever clearly dominates | Put all capacity on the most sensitive lever. Move on when it hits target, returns flatten, or new data changes the ranking. |
| 2–4 PMs, biggest lever unclear | Spend one quarter on quick wins across stages to learn where the leverage is, then consolidate on one lever. |
| Two levers share the same work (e.g. activation and paid conversion both need onboarding changes) | Work them together. Never split a team under five people across more than two levers. |
| 5+ PMs, mostly self-serve | Teams by funnel stage: acquisition, activation, monetisation/retention. |
| 5+ PMs, sales-assisted revenue matters | Teams by revenue type: new, retained, expansion. |
| Teams fight over shared engineers, data or experiment tooling | Add a horizontal growth platform team that serves the lever teams. |

## Rules

- Growth reports to the product org, not marketing. It must be able to change the product. Marketing stays a partner for channels and content.
- When priorities shift, move people between levers. Don't re-org.
- Every experiment carries a guardrail metric, so short-term wins don't erode the product.
- Track inputs (experiments shipped, hypotheses tested) as well as the output metric.
- Get an executive sponsor before forming the team, or it dies in the next reorg.

## Failure modes

- **Growth as a side project.** A PM doing growth part-time loses to every feature deadline.
- **Growth without data.** If you can't measure the lever, the first work item is instrumentation (`plg-data-setup`).
- **Too many metrics.** A team that owns fifteen numbers moves none.
- **Growth theatre.** Testing button colours when the value proposition is broken.
