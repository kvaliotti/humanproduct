# Audience Segment Research

Output: `strategic-research/02-audience-segments.md`. Follow `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/plain-writing.md`.

## Steps

1. Read `strategic-research/01-industry-process-map.md` if it exists and use its workflow steps and terms.
2. Run 5 to 10 web searches: review sites (G2, Capterra, app stores), Reddit and forums, case studies, job posts. Look for who complains, who pays, and what triggers them to look for a solution.
3. Write the file.

## Sections

1. **Segments at a glance:** a table with 3 to 5 segments. Columns: segment, how to spot them (something observable: company size, team, tool they use, event that just happened), what they're trying to get done, what they use today, how much they care (High / Medium / Low, with the reason).
2. **Segment profiles:** for each segment, 4 bullets:
   - **Trigger:** the event that makes them start looking.
   - **Top pains:** up to 3, each with a source.
   - **Who's involved:** who uses it, who pays, who can block it.
   - **What would make them switch:** in their words where possible.
3. **Who to go after first:** one segment and why, in 3 sentences. Name the runner-up.
4. **What we don't know**
5. **Sources**

Rules:
- Name segments by their situation, not by persona names ("Agencies managing 10+ client accounts", not "Agency Alex").
- Segments must differ in what they need or will pay, not just in size.
- Quote real people from reviews and forums where you can, with a link.
